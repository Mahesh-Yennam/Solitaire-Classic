🃏 Klondike Solitaire Rules & Strategy

🏛️
Foundations (Top-Right): Build each suit from Ace up to King (A, 2, 3... J, Q, K). Fill all four to win!
🎴
Tableau Columns (Bottom): Build downward in alternating colors (Red 7 on Black 8). Only a King can fill an empty column.
👆
One-Tap Smart Move: Tap or click any face-up card to automatically send it to the best legal foundation or tableau spot. Double-click to auto-send to foundation.
⚡
Autocomplete: When all face-down cards on the board are flipped over, click Autocomplete to finish the game with a single touch!

Desktop Controls: Drag and drop cards with mouse, or click cards for smart-moves. Press Ctrl+Z to Undo, H for Hint, and P to Pause.
Mobile Controls: Drag cards smoothly with your finger or tap cards to auto-position them instantly.
